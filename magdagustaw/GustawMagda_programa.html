Para Magda · For Magda

What to expect

🎯 One clear goal per class

Each class ends with something Gustaw can really say, and a short game where he talks without a script.

🧭 Flexible, not fixed

The plan is a map, not a contract. After every class I adjust the next one to what he found easy or hard.

🏠 Five minutes at home

Each class has a small task. At home you only need to ask him the questions in Spanish and celebrate his answers. No correcting needed.

🇪🇸 Spanish from Spain

Words like zumo, patata, cole and merienda sit alongside what he already learns in his weekly class with his teacher from Guatemala.

⭐ Progress you can see

Every class ends with Gustaw checking his own “superpowers”: what he can now do on his own, out loud. Stars, missions and a mistake bank keep what is hard coming back until it is easy.

🔊 A model to repeat after

Every key sentence can be tapped to be heard, so he always has real Spanish from Spain to listen to and repeat.
