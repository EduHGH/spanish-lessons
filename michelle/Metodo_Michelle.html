How we work together

Chunks

Conversational chunks

No isolated vocabulary lists or heavy grammar rules. You practise ready-made phrases like “¿Me puedes poner un poco más de…?” so whole sentences come out at once.

Scenes

Low-pressure, predictable scenes

The same daily situations come back again and again until they become second nature.

Your 50-minute class

Every class follows the same rhythm, so you always know what comes next.

  • Instant recall: rapid-fire matching of real situations to Spanish chunks
  • Sound and intonation lab: pronunciation and rhythm
  • Immersive role-play: the bakery, the shop, the phone call, with encouraging coaching
  • Take-away: key phrases to use out loud in Spain

Total: 50 minutes

The music of Spanish

In the sound lab we work on rhythm and melody. The tune of a sentence tells people whether you are telling or asking: the voice falls when you tell and rises when you ask.

What else you will be able to say

Beyond the five priority points, each class quietly adds a little more grammar inside the role-play. You never study it on its own: it arrives inside the phrases you practise. Here is a taste of what it unlocks.

Introductions

Say who you are

Me llamo Michelle. ¿Cómo te llamas?My name is Michelle. What’s your name?

Hablo poco español, pero estudio mucho.I speak little Spanish, but I study a lot.

Shopping and services

Ask for what you need

¿Puedo pagar con tarjeta?Can I pay by card?

Busco el queso, pero no lo encuentro.I’m looking for the cheese, but I can’t find it.

Questions

Ask and point things out

¿A qué hora abren?What time do you open?

Hay una fuga de agua.There’s a water leak.

Problems and health

Describe how things are

Me duelen los pies.My feet hurt.

Este jarabe es muy fuerte.This syrup is very strong.

Plans

Plan and put things in order

Mañana va a venir el fontanero.The plumber is going to come tomorrow.

Primero firmo aquí y luego pago.First I sign here and then I pay.

Likes

Share what you like

Me gusta correr y me gustan los libros de aventuras.I like running and I like adventure books.

A mí me gusta leer. ¿Y a ti?I like reading. And you?
