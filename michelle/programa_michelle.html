Prioridades del test inicial

Five points from Michelle’s initial test. They take priority: each one is introduced in classes 1–5 and then comes back in later scenes until it is automatic.

Gramática A1 por clase

Besides the five priority points, every class adds two or three A1 points from the ESCOLAR A1 grammar catalogue. Each one is kept because it appears in that class’s take-away phrases or in closely related ones.

Cómo trabajamos

Goal: stop building sentences word by word and start recalling whole phrases automatically.

Frases completas

You learn ready-made chunks, like “Para mí, un café con leche, por favor”, so whole sentences come out at once.

Situaciones predecibles

The same everyday scenes come back again and again (café, shop, phone call) until they feel automatic.

Práctica con juego

Quick-fire roleplays, mini-simulations and visual prompts. No dry drills, and Spanglish is welcome.

Gramática prioritaria

The five points from the initial test come first. Each is drilled as a ready-made chunk in the 10-minute Recuerdo rápido and recycled in later scenes. The rest of the A1 grammar rides inside the roleplay, one chunk at a time.
