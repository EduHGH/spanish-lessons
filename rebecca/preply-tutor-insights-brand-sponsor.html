01 · Executive summary

Five findings that should shape the tutor-relations roadmap

Ranked by expected influence on tutor satisfaction and retention.

01

Craft and student outcomes are the heart of tutor pride

45% of posts are about teaching craft and 38% about student outcomes — far ahead of income talk (7%). Tutors stay for mastery and meaning: brand programs and communications should celebrate how tutors teach, not only what they earn.

02

Recognition is the highest-octane engagement fuel

Recognition & badge posts earn 59 avg likes vs a 42 baseline (+41%) — the strongest resonance on the wall. Renewal stories follow at 52. Cheap to scale, deeply on-brand: recognition architecture is the top engagement lever.

03

The first 30 days are the emotional choke point

16% of posters are newcomers — and their posts get the lowest resonance (28 avg likes). The empty-calendar wait and self-doubt dominate their pain points. Newcomers need fast first wins and peer warmth precisely when the community gives them least attention.

04

Renewals are the growth engine tutors brag about

54 tutors (19%) credit renewals and long-term students for their year — the most bragged-about practical achievement. The habits behind their retention are codifiable and teachable.

05

Tutors are telling us exactly what to build

Their asks cluster around trainings, reusable templates, scheduling control, feedback rituals and community events — plus one explicit product request: bring back group classes.

“You don't have to feel completely confident before you begin. Sometimes, confidence comes because you begin.”

— Anonymous Tutor · 72 likes · the most re-readable line on the wall

05 · Deep dives

Eight themes that decide the tutor experience

For each: what tutors actually said (verbatim quotes), why it matters to the Brand Sponsor, and what to do about it.

Teaching Craft & Methodology

127 tutors · 45% ♥ 42.2 avg

Tutors define success by how well they teach — not just by what they earn.

Why it matters to the Brand Sponsor

Brand stories should celebrate pedagogy and personal method, not only income outcomes. Craft content also feeds the template library tutors keep asking for.

Act on it
  • →Co-create a lesson-template library with top contributors
  • →Feature 'method behind the magic' stories in tutor comms

Student Outcomes

107 tutors · 38% ♥ 46.1 avg

Student breakthroughs are the emotional engine of the community.

Why it matters to the Brand Sponsor

Every breakthrough story is organic proof of platform value. A student-outcome storytelling engine turns tutors into your most credible advocates.

Act on it
  • →Launch a monthly 'Student Breakthrough' spotlight
  • →Collect outcome consent stories for brand campaigns

Confidence & Mindset

85 tutors · 30% ♥ 39.5 avg

The invisible barrier: shyness, imposter feelings, fear of starting.

Why it matters to the Brand Sponsor

Confidence is the gate to retention: tutors who survive self-doubt stay. Normalizing the struggle in official comms reduces early churn more than any feature.

Act on it
  • →Publish 'What I wish I knew' first-90-days series
  • →Add peer encouragement prompts to newcomer journeys

Renewals & Long-term Students

54 tutors · 19% ♥ 52.4 avg

Renewals are the growth engine tutors brag about — and the highest-resonance practical theme.

Why it matters to the Brand Sponsor

Long-term students are the true measure of tutor-business health. The habits of top retainers are a reusable playbook waiting to be codified.

Act on it
  • →Co-create a renewal playbook with the 54 renewal storytellers
  • →Recognize first-renewal and 1-year student milestones

Recognition & Badges

29 tutors · 10% ♥ 59.3 avg

Super Tutor streaks and top-% badges are the strongest status currency on the wall.

Why it matters to the Brand Sponsor

At 59 avg likes, recognition posts out-resonate everything else. Recognition architecture is your cheapest, highest-leverage engagement lever.

Act on it
  • →Expand milestones (first renewal, 100th lesson, streaks)
  • →Make badge criteria transparent and celebratable

First Student & Early Wins

40 tutors · 14% ♥ 33.4 avg

The first booking unlocks everything — emotionally and financially.

Why it matters to the Brand Sponsor

Time-to-first-student is the single most emotionally loaded moment in the tutor journey. Every day shaved off compounds into retention.

Act on it
  • →Run first-student fast-track sprints for newcomers
  • →Celebrate 'my first student' moments publicly

Onboarding & Profile Setup

35 tutors · 12% ♥ 29.3 avg

Profile polish and intro videos are table stakes — but they gate everything else.

Why it matters to the Brand Sponsor

Low resonance (29 avg likes) means tutors see onboarding as hygiene, not joy. Remove friction fast, then get out of the way.

Act on it
  • →48h human review of intro videos and profiles
  • →A/B test profile checklists with completion tracking

Hardship & Resilience

16 tutors · 6% ♥ 58.6 avg

For a meaningful minority, teaching on Preply is a lifeline through crisis.

Why it matters to the Brand Sponsor

War, displacement, illness, power cuts — these stories earn the community's deepest respect (59 avg likes). A visible support program is a brand-values statement competitors cannot copy.

Act on it
  • →Stand up a Tutor Hardship Support Program
  • →Tell these stories with dignity (with consent) in brand channels

08 · Action plan

12 recommendations, three horizons

Each recommendation cites its evidence from the wall and the metric that proves it worked. Start with the quick wins — they fund the credibility of the strategic bets.

⚡

Quick wins

0–30 days · low lift, immediate signal

Q1
Low effort High impact

Launch a “First 30 Days” fast-track

Guided onboarding sprint: profile & intro-video clinic with human feedback within 48 hours, plus a first-trial checklist.

Evidence from the wall

16% of wall posts come from newcomers; the empty-calendar struggle is the most repeated pain family (17 tutors); onboarding is mentioned in 12% of posts.

Success looks like

Median days to first trial · % of new tutors active in week 4

Q2
Low effort High impact

Turn recognition into an always-on engine

Monthly spotlights for first renewal, 100th lesson, 1-year student anniversaries and Super Tutor streaks — pinned and amplified.

Evidence from the wall

Recognition posts earn the highest community resonance on the wall: 59 avg likes vs 42 baseline (+41%).

Success looks like

Engagement rate on recognition posts · % tutors reaching first renewal

Q3
Low effort Medium impact

Publish the “What I wish I knew” series

Curated struggle-to-win stories from real tutors, targeted at tutors in their first 6 months.

Evidence from the wall

30% of posts carry a confidence/mindset theme and 17% are struggle-to-win arcs — newcomers see veteran wins, rarely the doubts behind them.

Success looks like

Reach among tutors <6 months · sentiment shift in newcomer posts

🧱

Core programs

30–90 days · structural improvements

C1
Medium effort High impact

Stand up tutor-to-tutor mentorship

Structured buddy program matching veterans with newcomers, with recognition for mentors.

Evidence from the wall

Veterans have the highest resonance (54 avg likes) but are only 10% of wall voices; “newer tutors now message me for advice” earned 58 likes.

Success looks like

Match rate · mentee week-8 activity vs control group

C2
Medium effort High impact

Build the community template library

Reusable lesson templates and resources co-created with top tutors, with contributor credit and quality curation.

Evidence from the wall

“Reinventing the wheel every lesson” is a recurring pain; templates/resources are among the most frequent tutor asks; craft is the #1 theme (45%).

Success looks like

Monthly active users of the library · self-reported prep-time saved

C3
Low effort High impact

Codify the renewal playbook

Interview the 54 renewal storytellers and turn their habits (goal-checks, personalization, progress tracking) into a guide + in-product nudges.

Evidence from the wall

Renewals & long-term students is the highest-resonance practical theme (52 avg likes) and the clearest proxy of tutor-business health.

Success looks like

First-renewal rate within 90 days · 6-month student retention

C4
Medium effort Medium impact

Ritualize goals & progress checks

Encourage a goal-setting + small-win celebration ritual in lessons via prompts, templates and comms.

Evidence from the wall

Tutors themselves recommend goal-checks, feedback loops and tracking small progress more than any other behavior; student outcomes are the #2 theme (38%).

Success looks like

% of lessons with a stated goal · correlation of ritual use with renewal

🚀

Strategic bets

3–12 months · brand-defining moves

S1
High effort High impact

Pilot cohort & group experiences

Bring back group classes in a modern format: cohorts, conversation clubs, exam-prep circles.

Evidence from the wall

“Bring back group classes” is an explicit ask on the wall, and its removal is remembered as a loss; community formats drive belonging.

Success looks like

Cohort enrollment & completion · renewal rate of participants

S2
High effort High impact

Create a Tutor Hardship Support Program

Emergency support for tutors in crisis regions: connectivity kits, equipment, fee relief, community fund.

Evidence from the wall

6% of posts describe teaching through war, displacement, illness or power cuts — and they earn the second-highest resonance (59 avg likes).

Success looks like

Tutors retained through hardship · response time ≤ 7 days

S3
Medium effort Medium impact

Open niche specialization hubs

Dedicated spaces and tracks for medical language, kids & teens, exam prep and rare languages.

Evidence from the wall

Niche specialists earn above-average resonance (55 avg likes) and report stronger differentiation and pricing power.

Success looks like

Hub participation · income delta of niche tutors vs baseline

S4
Medium effort Medium impact

Run a pricing-confidence program

Guidance on positioning, raising prices and communicating value — with reassurance data on price-elasticity.

Evidence from the wall

Fear of raising prices appears repeatedly in pain points; income-transformation stories are among the most resonant (54 avg likes).

Success looks like

% of tutors with data-informed pricing · trial stability after increases

S5
Medium effort Medium impact

Ship an AI co-pilot toolkit — with a clear policy

Curated AI tools for lesson prep and feedback, plus plain-language policy on AI in reviews and learning.

Evidence from the wall

Tutors embrace AI as a co-pilot (7% theme) but flag anxieties such as “AI writing reviews”; ambiguity is the risk, capability is the opportunity.

Success looks like

Toolkit adoption · AI-related concern mentions on community walls

10 · Method & caveats

How to read this report (and how not to)

Method

  • 1.Corpus: all 281 public posts from the Progress Wall (Preply Online Teaching Conference 2026), including full original texts.
  • 2.Each post was coded on four dimensions: journey stage, themes (fixed 16-code taxonomy), sentiment, plus pain points, suggestions and the most representative quote.
  • 3.Coding was LLM-assisted and cross-validated with keyword checks on the original texts (e.g. 64% of posts touch income, 27% confidence language, 16% community/event references).
  • 4.Community likes (11,819 total) were used as a resonance proxy: a measure of what other tutors celebrate, not of business outcomes.
  • 5.Privacy: all tutor names are anonymized as “Anonymous Tutor” and no profile links are included, so the report can be shared safely beyond the community team.

Caveats

  • ·Self-selected sample. Wall posters are engaged tutors; silent, struggling or churned tutors are invisible here. Expect the true pain to be larger than shown.
  • ·Positive skew. A progress-celebration context (with a contest) inflates celebration (64%) — read pain families as floors, not ceilings.
  • ·Single event. One conference wall, one day, one audience; seasonal and market mix are not represented.
  • ·25% unspecified tenure. Add a tenure field to wall posts to sharpen the journey view next time.
  • ·Recommendations are hypotheses. Validate against product and CRM data before committing OKRs.
